## LETTER-002: Last Week in CTM

**Email ID:** `199d924153072a94`
**Letter Date:** `2025-10-12`

Ola,

Last week in CTM! I leave next Wednesday (the 15th) for Porto Alegre. This
week has been pretty interesting. Elder Röesti's district got here, so he's
no longer with us.  He was with us for a week because he was waiting for
his visa in his home mission (alpine).  It's weird seeing him with his
district, because that district is definitely worse, as no district can be
as good as the one I ( and formerly Elder Röesti) have.

We signed a contract this week saying that we would only speak in Portugese
for the remainder of our time in the CTM. It's pretty hard, as all the
newer districts speak mostly in English, and my Portuguese is not that
good. One funny part about speaking only in Portuguese is that when any of
us speak in English, they have to do a fubeca dance, and it's pretty funny.

Elder Roberts taught us a Haka because another New Zealand Elder was
leaving and his district wanted to do the Haka for their teachers. It was
pretty cool, but we didn't have much time to practice, so we are practicing
more this week to do it for our teacher. One of my teachers left this week
because she's getting married, and she was my favorite teacher. Hopefully i
make it through the week without defenestrating myself (or in portuguese,
me defenestrato).

Ate logo,
Elder Nelson

## LETTER-003: First real week as a missionary

**Email ID:** `19a07dde79844f97`
**Letter Date:** `2025-10-21`

Olá!
I'm finally out of the CTM. It was really hard saying goodbye to my friends
in the CTM but I'm excited to be in a new place. We left the CTM at 2:30 in
the morning, so me and Elder Robert's just stayed up all night drinking
Guarana.

My first area is called Itacolomi. It's about 30 minutes from Porto Alegre.
The area is pretty big, but we only proselytize in a small area (outlined
in blue). On Friday we pretty much walked by every house. It's all right, I
like the members, but no one really wants to talk to us. My Portuguese is
also pretty bad, so it's kinda hard to understand what people are talking
about when I talk to them. The food here is pretty good though, I really
like churrasco and chimarrao.  It  isn't as bad as people say it is (I just
bought a Chimarrao cup so I can make my own). The pizza here has chocolate
in the crusts and it's pretty yummy. There's an Açai place right across the
street, and that's pretty good. I baptized someone on Sunday, so that was
pretty exciting. I messed up the prayer twice, but I eventually got it.
Hopefully my Portuguese improves this week.

Elder Nelson

## LETTER-004: They call me the alemão

**Email ID:** `19a4fdb46a2dede4`
**Letter Date:** `2025-11-04`

Olá,
Met this guy this week who was working in his yard, and when we asked him
was his name was, he told us that people called him the alemão (the
german), and then we were like, "dude, half of your street is German
descendants, nobody actually calls you that, what's your real name?" And
then he told us his real name, which we promptly forgot, and now we just
refer to him as the alemão because it's funnier than finding out his real
name again.

Halloween is sorta a thing here in Itacolomi. I saw decorations, and kids
dressed up for school, and in one of the neighborhoods, kids went trick or
treating.

A lot of the people in our little area aren't really progressing, so that
kinda sucks. We're going to try a new place in our area, and see if we can
find more people. I understand about 50-60% of what people are saying,
which is a lot better then when I first got here, but hopefully the dom do
linguas will kick in soon and I'll understand pretty much everything.It was
Elder Wilson's birthday this week, and our ward threw him a surprise party.
A lady in our ward made us peanut butter cookies and they were delicious.
If you guys have any questions about Brasil, I would love to get an email
from you--my email is ethan.s.nelson@missionary.org.

Valeu,
Élder Nelson

## LETTER-005: Surprisingly Cold in Brasil

**Email ID:** `19a750dd5f5737cd`
**Letter Date:** `2025-11-11`

I thought it would be hot in Brasil by now, but I guess I was wrong. This
week has been the hottest/coldest I've been on the mission. It was 35
degrees (celcius, for some reason, Brasil uses the inferior metric system)
on Wednesday when I was in Cachoeirinha (different area than mine, we were
doing a division, in which two companionships swap, and i ended up in
Cachoeirinha) and I was baking from the heat. Then from Thursday-Saturday
it was raining and never got above 19 degrees. I was in my coat and still a
little cold (but that might have been because I was wet). I also had my
first Zone Conference this week. Zone Conference is when all the
missionaries in a certain area get together to listen to the President and
to do some practices with other missionaries. It was a lot of fun, and I
met some missionaries that knew some of the same people I did in Virginia.
This week I get to go to the Porto Alegre temple for the first time because
a guy that my companion baptized is going, and invited us to go with him.
People have already started setting up for Christmas (Natal in Brasil) and
some of the houses went all out, and really surprised my companion, as he
says that last year he didn't see any decorations.


Valeu,
Elder Nelson

Some people had told me that I should start keeping count of things I see
often in Brasil,
So....
I saw 26 Volkswagen beetles this week (and yes, me and my companion do play
punchbuggy)

## LETTER-006: I met an Apostle

**Email ID:** `19a9d587a19ae32f`
**Letter Date:** `2025-11-19`

Got to meet an apostle this week. Elder Soares came to my mission and I got
to hear him speak. Unfortunately for me, I am still learning Portuguese, so
I wasn't able to understand everything, but it was still really cool. I
also got to visit the Porto Alegre temple, because one of my companion's
recent converts was going. It was pretty cool.

One day while we were coming back from the church at night, we found a
couch on the side of the road, so we took it home. Unfortunately we don't
have enough space for it inside the house, and it's been raining a lot, but
the couch was already beat up before that, and it's still a good place to
sit at the end of the day when the night is cool, but the house is hot.

My Portuguese continues to get better, and I've gotten to the point where I
can teach the lessons pretty good. We had to go to the hospital this week
because Elder Wilson wasn't feeling good, and they made him get an
ultrasound. I teased him the whole ride home about how he's going to be a
father soon.

Tried sugarcane juice this week, not sure if I like it. Elder Wilson says
its usually better, and they add lime juice to it, so I think I just need
to find another place to try it.


Valeu,
Elder Nelson

## LETTER-008: 3 Months!!

Ola,
Finally made it 3 months! Now all i gotta do is make it through the next
21!  Things are going alright here, it's been a bit of a challenge with

Ola,
